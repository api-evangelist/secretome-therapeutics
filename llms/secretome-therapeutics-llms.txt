# Secretome Therapeutics

> Clinical-stage biotechnology company (formerly NeoProgen, founded 2018) developing allogeneic,
> off-the-shelf regenerative therapies derived from neonatal cardiac progenitor cells (nCPCs).
> Lead candidate STM-01 and the cell-free secretome STM-2 are in Phase 1 study for heart failure
> with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF), dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM) and Duchenne muscular
> dystrophy associated cardiomyopathy.
